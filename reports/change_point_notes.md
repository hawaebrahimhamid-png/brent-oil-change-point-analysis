# Change Point Model Notes

## What is a Change Point?

A change point is a point in time where the statistical behavior of a time series changes significantly. For Brent oil prices, a change point may indicate a major shift caused by economic, political, or global events.

## Why Use Bayesian Methods?

Bayesian change point analysis estimates the probability that a change occurred at different points in time. Unlike traditional methods, it provides uncertainty estimates and allows prior knowledge to be incorporated into the model.

## Expected Model Output

The Bayesian model is expected to produce:
- The estimated change point(s).
- The probability of each change point.
- Estimated price behavior before and after each change.

## Limitations

- The model identifies statistical changes but cannot determine their exact causes.
- Change points may not correspond perfectly to real-world events.
- Results depend on the assumptions and prior distributions used in the model.
- External factors affecting oil prices may not be fully captured.
